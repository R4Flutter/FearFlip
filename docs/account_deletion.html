Delete your FearFlip account & data

Developer: Raj Naik · App: FearFlip (com.rajnaik.fearflip)

You can delete your FearFlip account and all associated data at any time. There are two ways to do it.

Option 1 — in the app (fastest)

  1. Open FearFlip.
  2. Go to the account / settings screen.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Your account is removed immediately and a server cleanup job erases the data listed below. If your sign-in is too old to delete on the spot, the request is queued and completed by the cleanup job automatically.

Option 2 — by email

If you can no longer open the app, email naikraj116@gmail.com from the address linked to your Google sign-in with the subject “FearFlip account deletion”. We action requests within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your Firebase Authentication account (Google / guest sign-in).
  • Your global & survival leaderboard entries and best scores.
  • Your run history.
  • Your “Remove Ads” purchase / entitlement record.

Note: your Google Play purchase receipt is retained by Google Play, not by us, for legal and refund purposes. Anonymised, aggregated analytics and crash logs that cannot be tied back to you may be retained.
