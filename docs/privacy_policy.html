Privacy Policy

FearFlip Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-21

Overview

FearFlip uses Firebase and Google Mobile Ads to provide gameplay services, authentication, analytics, crash reporting, and monetization. This policy explains what data we process, why we process it, and the choices available to you.

Data We Collect

  • Account identifiers for guest or Google sign-in users.
  • Gameplay events and progression metrics.
  • Crash logs, device diagnostics, and performance metrics.
  • Ad delivery signals required by Google Mobile Ads.

Why We Collect Data

  • To authenticate players and keep account continuity.
  • To operate leaderboards and progression systems.
  • To diagnose crashes, errors, and performance issues.
  • To serve ads and measure ad delivery outcomes.

Third-Party Services

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Cloud Firestore
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)

Ad Consent and Personalization

Where required by law, we request your consent for ad personalization using the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can manage your consent choices inside the app via Settings - Privacy and Consent.

If you decline consent, ads may be limited or disabled depending on your region and Google policy requirements.

Your Choices

  • Manage ad consent in Settings - Privacy and Consent.
  • Request account deletion in Settings - Account.
  • Contact us with privacy questions or requests.

Account Deletion

You can request deletion in-app or through our web form. Deletion requests cover Firebase Authentication identity, leaderboard identity, cloud progress, and related gameplay records where technically possible.

  • In-app path: Settings - Account - Delete Account and Data
  • Web path: https://fearflipgame.com/account-deletion

Data Retention

We retain data only as long as needed to operate the game, meet legal obligations, and support player requests. Deletion requests are honored as quickly as possible, subject to technical and legal constraints.

Children's Privacy

FearFlip is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will act on the request.

Security

We use industry-standard safeguards to protect your data, but no method of transmission or storage is 100 percent secure.

Contact

For privacy-related requests, contact: privacy@fearflipgame.com

This policy may be updated from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the date above and, when required, provide additional notice.
