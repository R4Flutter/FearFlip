# FearFlip Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-04-12

FearFlip uses Firebase and Google Mobile Ads to provide gameplay services, authentication, analytics, crash reporting, and monetization.

## Data we process
- Authentication identifiers (guest and linked Google sign-in accounts)
- Gameplay events and progression metrics
- Crash diagnostics and device metadata for stability
- Ad mediation and ad delivery signals required by Google Mobile Ads

## Why we process data
- To authenticate players and keep account continuity
- To operate leaderboards and progression systems
- To diagnose crashes, errors, and performance issues
- To serve ads and track ad delivery outcomes

## Third-party services
- Firebase Authentication
- Cloud Firestore
- Firebase Analytics
- Firebase Crashlytics
- Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)

## User choices
- Users can opt out or manage ad consent where required by law (UMP flow)
- Users can request account deletion through support channels

## Contact
For privacy-related requests, contact: privacy@fearflipgame.com

## Hosting note for Play Console
This policy must be hosted on a publicly accessible HTTPS URL and that URL must be submitted in Play Console.
